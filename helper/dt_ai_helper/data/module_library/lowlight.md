# lowlight vision (lowlight)
group: effect | display-referred | typical position: late, creative stage
synonyms: night, night vision, scotopic, purkinje, blue shift, moonlight, twilight, dark scene, low light look
purpose: Simulates human scotopic (low-light) vision, where colour perception fades and
  the eye shifts toward blue, blending that response with normal photopic
  vision.
use_when: you want a night scene to read as it looked to the eye rather than as a
  brightly-lit daylight rendering; you want a moonlight or twilight look with
  the characteristic blue shift.
