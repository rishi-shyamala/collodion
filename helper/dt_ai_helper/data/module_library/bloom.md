# bloom (bloom)
group: effect | display-referred | typical position: late, creative stage
synonyms: bloom, glow, halation, dreamy, light spread, soft glow, highlight bleed, romantic, hazy light
purpose: Spreads light out of the brightest areas into their surroundings, emulating
  the halation of film and old lenses.
use_when: highlights should glow and bleed into adjacent areas for a dreamy or romantic
  look; you want film-like halation around light sources. diffuse or sharpen's
  bloom preset is the more controllable modern alternative.
