# color contrast (colorcontrast)
group: color | display-referred | typical position: after tone mapping
synonyms: color contrast, saturation, green magenta, blue yellow, lab contrast, chroma contrast, color separation
purpose: Increases or decreases contrast along the green-magenta and blue-yellow axes
  of Lab space independently, which reads as a targeted saturation change on
  those axes.
use_when: you want more colour separation without a global saturation boost; the image
  needs a specific push along the green-magenta or blue-yellow axis. color
  balance rgb is the modern, better-behaved alternative.
