# enlarge canvas (enlargecanvas)
group: geometry | output | typical position: very late, after crop
synonyms: add canvas, extend canvas, padding, margin, letterbox, pad to square, background color, expand frame
purpose: Adds extra canvas around the image in a chosen colour without scaling the
  photograph, changing the output aspect ratio by padding rather than cropping.
use_when: you need a specific output aspect ratio (square for social media, 16:9 for a
  slideshow) without losing any of the photograph; you want padding to place
  text or a caption. framing does the same plus a keyline frame.
