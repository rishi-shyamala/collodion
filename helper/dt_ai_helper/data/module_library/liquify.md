# liquify (liquify)
group: correction | any | typical position: after demosaic, geometric stage
synonyms: liquify, warp, distort, push pixels, slim, reshape, straighten a line, portrait retouch, bulge, pinch, twirl
purpose: Locally warps the image with point, line and curve tools that push, twist or
  pinch pixels, without changing their colour.
use_when: a portrait needs subtle reshaping; a straight line is bowed and lens
  correction cannot fix it; you want a deliberate distortion effect. It is slow
  on large images and its effect is baked relative to the current crop.
