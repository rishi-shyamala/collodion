# dither or posterize (dither)
group: effect | output | typical position: very late, output stage
synonyms: banding, dither, posterize, gradient banding, 8 bit, sky banding, quantization, noise dither, color reduction
purpose: Adds dithering noise to break up quantisation banding when the image is
  reduced to a lower bit depth, or deliberately posterises the image to a small
  number of levels as a creative effect.
use_when: smooth skies or gradients show visible banding in 8-bit exports; you want a
  deliberate posterised or reduced-palette look.
