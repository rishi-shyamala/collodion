# soften (soften)
group: effect | display-referred | typical position: late, creative stage
synonyms: soft focus, glow, dreamy, portrait glow, orton, diffusion filter, romantic, hazy, soft filter
purpose: Blends a blurred, brightened, saturated copy of the image over the original to
  produce a soft-focus diffusion-filter effect.
use_when: a portrait or floral image wants a romantic soft-focus glow; you are emulating
  a diffusion filter or an Orton effect. diffuse or sharpen's orton and bloom
  presets give more control.
