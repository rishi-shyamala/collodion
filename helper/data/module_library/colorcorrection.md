# color correction (colorcorrection)
group: color | display-referred | typical position: after tone mapping
synonyms: split toning, color cast, tint shadows, tint highlights, ab pad, global saturation, color correction, sepia
purpose: Applies a colour cast via a Lab a/b colour pad, either uniformly or split
  between shadows and highlights, and can adjust global saturation.
use_when: you want a quick global tint or a simple split-tone; you are reproducing a
  legacy edit. color balance rgb's 4 ways tab supersedes it for modern work.
