# colorize (colorize)
group: effect | display-referred | typical position: late, creative stage
synonyms: colorize, tint, sepia, monochrome tint, duotone, uniform color, hand colored, single color wash
purpose: Replaces the image's colour with a single chosen hue and saturation while
  keeping its lightness, producing a uniform monochrome tint.
use_when: you want a sepia, cyanotype or single-colour wash over the whole image; you
  need a flat tint as a layer for blending.
