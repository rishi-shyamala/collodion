# filmic (deprecated) (filmic)
group: tone | deprecated | typical position: legacy stacks only
synonyms: filmic v1, old filmic, deprecated, legacy, tone mapping, dynamic range
purpose: The first-generation filmic tone mapper, which worked on luminance rather than
  on RGB ratios and produced more colour shift in the extremes.
use_when: only for history stacks made before filmic rgb existed; use filmic rgb,
  sigmoid or agx for new work.
